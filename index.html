How he became Lumen

Four moments, one light

01
Origin
The Boy Who Chased Light

From childhood, Aarav Menon was fascinated by light. Where others saw only illumination, he saw energy, information, and the future of technology. Raised by an electrical engineer and a physics teacher, he dedicated himself to photonics and energy systems — chasing clean, powerful, controllable energy for humanity. That pursuit led him to a classified research program.

"Light is more than what your eyes can see." — Aarav's father
02
Project Helios
An Artificial Star

At 24, Aarav joined a classified program with an extraordinary objective: build an artificial star-like energy core capable of generating and controlling enormous photonic and electromagnetic energy. There was one unsolved problem: the human body was never designed to survive that amount of energy.

03
The Accident
Containment Failure
1 During a critical test, core levels spike beyond safe limits.
2 The emergency shutdown fails — the Helios Core is about to explode.
3 Instead of escaping, Aarav enters the chamber to trigger a manual release.
4 The core detonates — its central chamber merges with his body.

He should have died. Instead, his cells began absorbing and adapting to the energy — his body became the containment system.

04
The Awakening
Three Days Later

Aarav remained unconscious for three days. On the third night, the hospital lights failed and the monitors stopped — then a golden-white light began glowing from his chest. He raised his hand. Light poured from his fingertips. He could suddenly perceive far beyond the visible spectrum: infrared, ultraviolet, heat signatures, electromagnetic signals, hidden objects invisible to the human eye. Aarav Menon was no longer an ordinary human. He had become Lumen — the Living Light.

The Cost of Power

The Helios Core is not unlimited. Every use of his strongest abilities raises its temperature. If he pushes beyond the limit, Lumen could lose his life. Every time he chooses to save someone, he risks destroying himself.

Philosophy

"No one should be left in the dark."

Lumen's mission is to use his extraordinary photonic powers to protect people, respond to emergencies, and help those who have nowhere else to turn. He monitors distress signals from his headquarters, Astra-7, and acts whenever someone needs help.

His philosophy is:
"Power isn't about being the strongest. It's about being there when someone needs you."
— AARAV MENON
